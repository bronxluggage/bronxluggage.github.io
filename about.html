About Bronx Luggage

Travel gear should be as good-looking as it is tough.

Our Story

Built for the road,
designed in the Bronx.

Bronx Luggage began with a simple idea: travel gear should be as good-looking as it is tough. What started as a small shop in the Bronx is now a curated destination for luggage, bags and backpacks, and every piece is tested on real trips before it earns a place on our shelves.

We stock our own Bronx line alongside Samsonite, American Tourister, Delsey and Travelpro, so whether you need a cabin spinner for a weekend away or a full 28" case for a month abroad, you will find it here, with honest prices, clear specifications and reviews from real travellers.
